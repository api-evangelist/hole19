# Hole19 Golf

> Hole19 is a golf app for iOS, Android, and smartwatches used by 4.8M+ golfers worldwide. It provides GPS rangefinder distances, digital scorecards, shot tracking, performance statistics, and a handicap calculator across 42,000+ golf courses in 200+ countries, plus social features like live leaderboards (LivePlay) and a social feed. The core app is free, with two subscription tiers: Premium and Intelligence (the most advanced tier, which includes everything in Premium).

Hole19 Golf (Stat Track Technologies Lda) operates two mobile apps: **Hole19: Golf GPS & Range Finder** (on-course GPS, scoring, and stats) and **CORE Golf: Practice Drills** (structured practice plans and 100+ drills). Hole19 is also a social golf platform: golfers follow friends in a social feed, compete on live leaderboards with LivePlay, and share rounds and stats. The website also hosts a worldwide golf course directory with community reviews, a free golf handicap calculator, a golf glossary, and The 19th Hole magazine. Tagline: "However you play. Play More."
